Glide Cone Map

Glide cone visualization for aircraft location, altitude, glide ration and wind

Location can be choosed via dragging the cursor or clicking the map.

Used for KCAS to KTAS conversion.

Each row describes the glide ratio, glide speed (KCAS), and wind for the altitude range up to the next lower altitude block.
